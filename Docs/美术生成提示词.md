# 废土机械风美术生成提示词

下面是后续生成的可复用规格。本次尚未调用生图工具，提示词不是已验证的生成结果。AI 输出需按《像素美术规范》转换、校色与验收后才能导入。

## 1. 所有世界素材的共同前缀

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: source artwork for a Unity 2D side-scrolling pixel-art platform game.
Theme: abandoned industrial wasteland, small repair robots, rusted machinery.
Camera: strictly orthographic side view, no perspective, no isometric view.
Style: very simple readable silhouettes, large contiguous flat color regions,
two or three discrete tones per material, consistent upper-left lighting.
Use muted steel gray, rust brown, sand beige; sparing cyan energy accents.
Pixel specification: all final world assets share 32 native pixels per Unity
world unit. A 1 by 1 world-unit tile must become a native 32 by 32 pixel PNG.
Do not mix pixel scales or use tiny detailed areas inside coarse pixel art.
If showing pixel art, use a uniform square pixel grid, crisp grid-aligned edges.
Keep the source easy to downsample and palette-quantize at the target size.
Avoid: gradients, airbrush, bloom, blurred edges, antialiasing, reflections,
dithering, random texture noise, dense rust speckles, tiny scratches, text,
logos, watermarks, cast shadows outside the object, dramatic perspective.
The exact target resolution and palette will be enforced after generation;
do not add a frame, labels, rulers, or a painted checkerboard background.
```

十六进制色板作为视觉参考，后处理负责精确映射；仅写入提示不能保证颜色精确。生成单个对象时在工具参数中指定真实透明背景，不只靠文字要求。

## 2. 样板 A：机器人站姿原图

共同前缀之后加入：

```text
Primary request: one small friendly repair robot, full body, facing right,
neutral standing pose. Boxy steel head, one cyan visor, rust-orange torso,
two simple arms, two sturdy short mechanical legs. Compact proportions.
Composition: a single isolated character, centered, generous empty margins,
feet share a level baseline, no floor and no external shadow.
Native target canvas: 32x48 pixels; visible body about 24x40 pixels.
World canvas size: 1x1.5 units at 32 PPU. Preserve this intended proportion.
The visor, hands and feet must remain readable at that native size.
Use approximately 6 to 8 colors, plain opaque shapes with a simple dark outline.
Background: real transparent alpha.
```

后续动作基于已选站姿参考，保持头身比例、颜色、关节和脚底位置。最小动画集：待机 2 帧、跑步 4 帧、起跳/下降各 1 帧、冲刺 1 帧；死亡可先用颜色闪烁和像素碎片表现。动作表必须人工对齐后切片。

## 3. 样板 B：铁板地块

```text
Primary request: one square side-view industrial floor tile, heavy rusted
steel block, flat walkable horizontal top edge, simple dark metal interior.
Native target: exactly one 32x32 pixel tile, 1x1 world units at 32 PPU.
Composition: square tile filling its canvas; no perspective, no drop shadow.
Use broad steel-gray shapes, one restrained rust patch, at most two bolts.
Top surface must connect seamlessly with copies placed on either side.
Keep left and right edge patterns compatible. No decorative border frame.
Solid opaque tile; no scene, no other tiles, no text or labels.
```

此素材与透明对象不同，实心地块可用不透明背景。生成后必须检查边缘并重复平铺；必要时人工修改边缘像素，不能直接当无缝 Tile 使用。

## 4. 样板 C：能源碎片

```text
Primary request: one compact cyan energy cell collectible, simple angular
capsule in a dark steel casing, one clear cyan center, no glow outside it.
Native target canvas: 16x16 pixels, 0.5x0.5 world units at 32 PPU.
Single centered isolated object, small transparent margins, real alpha.
Use 4 to 5 flat colors, recognizable silhouette, no tiny circuitry or text.
```

护盾道具沿用尺寸，使用盾形外壳和青色核心；跳跃增强用警示黄色外壳和明显向上的几何箭头。分别生成单个对象，避免一张图里混入多个尺寸不一致的道具。

## 5. 样板 D：废弃工厂远景模块

```text
Primary request: one side-view abandoned factory background module,
simple silhouettes of a broken smokestack, broad tanks and thick pipes,
sparse sand-colored negative space, quiet industrial wasteland atmosphere.
Native target: 320x192 pixels, displayed at 10x6 world units with 32 PPU.
Every final logical pixel must use the same square grid as the character
and terrain. Express distance through low contrast, not a different pixel scale.
Mostly dark desaturated gray and muted sand colors, few large color regions.
No tiny windows, no atmospheric blur, no gradient sky, no foreground floor,
no character, no interactive-looking ledges, no text or dramatic light rays.
Use a solid muted background. Keep left and right silhouettes simple so
separate modules can be arranged along a longer level.
```

远景不用透明也可以；若后续拆成多层视差，需要各层单独请求透明输出，正式素材仍用 32 PPU 和缩放 1。

## 6. 直接生成像素图的试验追加词

```text
Render as clean low-resolution pixel art rather than a smooth illustration.
All edges must follow one consistent square pixel lattice. No subpixel details.
Use contiguous pixel clusters, limited flat palette, no dithering.
If the output is enlarged, enlarge every logical pixel by the exact same
integer factor with nearest-neighbor edges; no mixed-size faux pixel blocks.
```

这一路线先用于样板对比。如果网格混杂、细节难还原，回到简洁平涂原图路线，不连续堆叠“pixel art”词语期待自动修复。

## 7. 后续批次与记录

首批仅做四个样板。统一视觉后，依次生成地形变体、角色动作、巡逻敌人、检查点与终端、两种道具、少量装饰。UI 与中文文字留给 Unity 排版。

每个文件记录：素材 ID、版本、提示词、参考图、来源文件、是否透明、可见尺寸、正式画布、枢轴、转换算法与色板版本、验收结果。正式原生文件和整数倍预览分开命名，例如 hero_idle_00.png 与 hero_idle_00_preview_8x.png。
